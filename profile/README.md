# workfinds

[workfinds.app](https://workfinds.app) is a free job search site and app. It lists open jobs taken straight from employers' own career sites and hiring systems, so every job links to the real posting and you apply on the employer's own page. You can filter by the country you can work from, by remote, hybrid or onsite, by employment type, by posting date, and by listed salary.

## What we stand for

- **Only open jobs, linked to the employer's own posting.** We read company career sites, list what is open, and take closed jobs down. workfinds never applies for you.
- **No ads and no third-party trackers.** Results are ordered by relevance and recency, never by payment, and the site analytics use no cookies.
- **No AI mass auto-apply.** We don't add to the flood of job applications.
- **Your language.** The site and the apps come in 31 interface languages.
